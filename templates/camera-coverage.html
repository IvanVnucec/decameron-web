where the cameras come from

Every camera comes from OpenStreetMap, the free map of the world made by millions of volunteers. Think of it as Wikipedia, but for maps.

Each night we download all the fixed speed cameras mapped there, together with their speed limits, and build a new camera list from them. The map above shows the same list your decameron gets with its next update.

Mobile cameras, red-light cameras and average-speed sections are not included.

missing a camera? add it.

anyone can add a camera, and every decameron gets it with the next update

1

open OpenStreetMap

Create a free account on openstreetmap.org. New to it? Start with the beginner's guide.

2

add a point

Zoom to the camera, click edit, place a point on the road where the camera is, and choose “speed camera”. Add the limit as maxspeed, for example 50.

3

update your device

The next nightly build picks it up. Update decameron and the camera is on board.
