first setup

  1. connect the device to any USB-C power source
  2. place it on or near the dashboard, speaker facing down
  3. wait for the first GPS fix — about 30 seconds, GPS LED turns from red to green
  4. adjust the volume wheel if needed

no app, no pairing, no configuration. that is the whole setup.

update in your browser

hold the Update button on the right side with a paperclip, connect the device with a USB-C data cable, then click connect device. after it appears in the browser window, click flash device.

waiting for device.

manual update

  1. download the latest catchcam.uf2 file
  2. hold the Update button on the right side with a paperclip
  3. connect the device to the computer with a USB-C data cable
  4. it appears as a USB drive named RPI-RP2
  5. drag and drop catchcam.uf2 onto the drive
  6. wait 15–30 seconds — the device restarts
  7. unplug it and use it as usual
