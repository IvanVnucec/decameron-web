first setup

  1. place it

    On the dashboard, speaker facing down, where it can see the sky through the windscreen.

  2. plug it in

    Into any USB port or USB adapter in the car. You hear a start-up sound and “searching for a GPS signal”.

  3. wait for GPS

    Usually about 30 seconds; the first time can take a few minutes. You hear “GPS signal acquired” and the GPS LED turns green.

  4. set the volume

    With the wheel on the side. Then drive.

update the camera list

new cameras are added every day. updating once a month, or before a long trip, keeps your decameron up to date

{#- both ways to update are folds. when this browser can't do USB, js/updater.js greys out the browser one (its status line says why) and opens the manual one -#}

Works in Chrome, Edge and Opera on a computer. You need a USB-C data cable; some charging cables won't work.

  1. With a paperclip, press and hold the update button on the right side.
  2. Keep holding it while you connect decameron to the computer, then let go.
  3. Click connect device and choose RP2 Boot in the window that opens.
  4. Click update device and wait for “update complete”.

waiting for device.

{#- manual-update: FAQ answers link here, and site.js opens the fold on arrival -#}

  1. Download {{ firmware_file }} ({{ firmware_mb }} MB).
  2. Press and hold the update button with a paperclip, connect decameron to the computer, then let go.
  3. A USB drive called RPI-RP2 appears.
  4. Drag {{ firmware_file }} onto it.
  5. Wait 15–30 seconds: the drive disappears and decameron restarts with the new list.
