setup and update guide

1. Download the latest decameron.uf2 file from https://ivanvnucec.github.io/decameron/ 2. Hold the Update button (on the right side) with a paperclip. 3. Plug the device into your PC — it will appear as a USB drive. 4. Drag & drop the decameron.uf2 file onto it. 5. Wait 15–30 seconds — the device will restart with the new firmware.
