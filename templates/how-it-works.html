Decameron is a speed camera detector that uses a GNSS module to determine your speed and position. It then compares your position to a database of known speed camera locations stored on device.

On approaching a speed camera, Decameron will alert you with a voice message spefiying the camera speed limit and a visual LED alert.

getting started

  1. Connect the device to a USB-C power source.
  2. Place the device on or near the vehicle dashboard with the speaker facing downward.

And thats it. You can enjoy peacefull driving.

When you connect the device, you should hear the startup sound. If it doesn’t play, simply turn the volume rotary wheel clockwise or counterclockwise to adjust it to your liking. decameron has three LEDs that show different status information: - LED closest to the rotary wheel (System Status): Blinks green periodically when everything is working normally, and red when there’s an issue. - Middle LED (Blue): Flashes when a speed camera is detected. - Outer LED (GPS Fix): Red while searching for a GPS signal, green once a position is acquired. When first powered on, decameron needs about 30 seconds to acquire a GPS fix. After that, it maintains the signal. If it’s temporarily lost (e.g. in tunnels), it usually regains it in a matter of seconds. When a speed camera is detected, the device flashes the blue LED and gives an audio warning with the speed limit. Faster beeping means your current speed is above the camera’s limit, while slower beeping means you’re below it.
